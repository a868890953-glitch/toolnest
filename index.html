Class 9 Maths Formulas

Important formulas for quick revision and practice.

Number Systems

aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ
aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ
(aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ

Polynomials

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
a² - b² = (a-b)(a+b)

Coordinate Geometry

Point = (x, y)
x-coordinate = Abscissa
y-coordinate = Ordinate

Lines & Angles

Linear pair = 180°
Angles around a point = 360°
Vertically opposite angles are equal

Triangles

Sum of angles = 180°
Area = ½ × base × height
Pythagoras: c² = a² + b²

Quadrilaterals

Sum of angles = 360°
Rectangle Area = l × b
Square Area = a²

Circles

Diameter = 2r
Circumference = 2πr
Area = πr²

Heron's Formula

s = (a+b+c)/2
Area = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)]

Surface Areas & Volumes

Cuboid Volume = lbh
Cube Volume = a³
Cylinder Volume = πr²h

Statistics

Mean = Sum of observations ÷ Number of observations

Probability

P(E) = Favourable outcomes ÷ Total outcomes

Area

Triangle = ½bh
Rectangle = lb
Parallelogram = bh
