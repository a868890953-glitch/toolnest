About Our Calculators & Tools

Professional, simple and practical tools for everyday calculations.

Age Calculator

The ToolNest Age Calculator helps users determine their age from a date of birth and a selected calculation date. Instead of simply subtracting years, the calculator considers months and days so that the result can provide a more precise age breakdown. It can be useful for students, form filling, planning events, checking age eligibility and everyday date-related calculations. The tool is designed to make date calculations quick and understandable without requiring manual calendar calculations.

EMI Calculator

The EMI Calculator is designed to estimate monthly loan payments using the principal amount, annual interest rate and loan tenure. It uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula and provides the estimated monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment amount. This makes it useful for understanding the mathematical cost of a loan before making financial decisions. Actual loan payments can vary depending on lender policies, fees, taxes, interest-rate changes and other terms, so the calculator should be treated as an estimation tool rather than a financial guarantee.

BMI Calculator

The BMI Calculator calculates Body Mass Index from height and weight using the standard BMI formula. BMI is a population-level screening measure and does not directly diagnose an individual's health or describe body composition. For adults aged 20 and above, commonly used BMI categories can provide a general reference. For children and teenagers under 20, BMI needs to be interpreted using age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age percentiles. ToolNest therefore includes an important reminder not to apply adult BMI categories to people under 20. The calculator is intended for general information and should not replace professional medical advice.

CGPA Calculator

The CGPA Calculator helps students calculate the average of their grade points across multiple subjects. Users can enter grade points separated by commas and receive the average CGPA instantly. The tool can be useful for academic planning, checking results and understanding overall grade-point performance. Because schools, colleges and education boards may use different grading systems, users should confirm the official conversion rules applicable to their institution when converting CGPA into percentage or another grading format.

Marks Percentage Calculator

The Marks Percentage Calculator provides a simple way to calculate percentage from obtained marks and total marks. The standard formula is obtained marks divided by total marks multiplied by 100. Students can use it for individual examinations, assignments or overall marks calculations. The tool removes the need for manual arithmetic and provides the result immediately. It is especially useful when checking academic performance or preparing information for educational forms and records.

Discount Calculator

The Discount Calculator determines the discount amount and final price after a percentage discount is applied to an original price. It can be useful while comparing offers, understanding sale prices and performing everyday shopping calculations. For example, entering an original price and discount percentage allows the tool to show both the amount saved and the price after the discount. The calculation does not include additional taxes, delivery charges or other fees unless they are already included in the entered price.

Date Calculator

The Date Calculator helps users find the number of days between two dates. Date calculations can be useful for project planning, study schedules, deadlines, event planning, leave calculations and many other situations. ToolNest processes the selected dates and calculates their difference using JavaScript date functions. The result represents the mathematical difference between the selected calendar dates. Users should consider whether their particular situation requires inclusive counting, because some official forms and processes count both the starting and ending date.

Character Counter

The Character Counter is a useful writing and productivity tool that counts characters, characters without spaces, words, sentences and lines. It can help students, writers, content creators and anyone working with text limits. The counter updates automatically as text is entered, making it convenient for checking the length of an essay, description, application, social-media post or other written content. All calculations happen directly in the browser on this page, making the tool quick and convenient for ordinary text-counting tasks.

Case Converter

The Case Converter provides several text-formatting options, including uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case. It is useful when editing notes, preparing documents, cleaning copied text or changing the presentation of written content. Users can enter their text, choose a conversion option and copy the transformed result. The tool performs the conversion directly in the browser, allowing users to make quick formatting changes without installing additional software.
