How to Calculate Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a part of 100. You can calculate a percentage by dividing the obtained value by the total value and multiplying the result by 100.

Percentage = (Obtained Value ÷ Total Value) × 100

How to Use This Percentage Calculator?

  1. Enter the obtained value or number.
  2. Enter the total value.
  3. Click the Calculate button.
  4. Your percentage result will appear instantly.

Example

Suppose you scored 450 marks out of a total of 500 marks.

(450 ÷ 500) × 100 = 90%
