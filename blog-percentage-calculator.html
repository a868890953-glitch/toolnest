How to Calculate Percentage: Formula, Examples & Calculator

Percentage is one of the most commonly used mathematical concepts in everyday life. We use percentages to calculate marks, discounts, profits, increases, decreases and many other things.

What is a Percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred" and is represented using the % symbol.

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Value ÷ Total Value) × 100

Example

Suppose a student scores 450 marks out of 500.

Percentage = (450 ÷ 500) × 100

Percentage = 90%
